# TIMING.md — Project Studio 1.0

---

## THE RULE OF THIRDS (TIME)

30 seconds = 3 scenes. 10 seconds each. No exceptions.
60 seconds = 5 scenes. 12 seconds each.
90 seconds = 7 scenes. ~13 seconds each.

---

## SCENE TIMING ANATOMY

```
0.0s–0.5s   → Entry animation (elements spring in)
0.5s–8.5s   → Content display + narration
8.5s–9.5s   → Exit begins (elements prepare to leave)
9.5s–10.0s  → Transition to next scene
```

---

## AUDIO-VISUAL SYNC RULES

- Visual scene starts: 0.2s before narrator begins that scene's line
- Visual scene ends: 0.5s after narrator finishes that scene's line
- Teal element appears: exactly on the key word in narration — count frames from audio
- Character expression switches: 2 frames before the beat it matches

---

## WHAT TIMING.MD PROTECTS AGAINST

- Visuals racing ahead of voice (viewer gets lost)
- Voice finishing before visuals resolve (awkward silence)
- Scenes that are too short to read (under 6 seconds — never)
- Scenes that overstay (over 15 seconds in a reel — never)
