# MUSIC.md — Project Studio 1.0

---

## USAGE RULE

Music is optional per video. Temy decides in pre-production.
Default for 30s reels: no music — voice is enough.
Default for 60s+: subtle background music.

---

## VOLUME LEVELS

```
Background music under voice:  12–15% volume
Music during intro (no voice): 60% volume
Music fade in:                 1 second
Music fade out:                last 2 seconds of video
```

---

## ASSET LOCATION

`/attachments/music/`
Files named by mood: calm.mp3, curious.mp3, reveal.mp3, neutral.mp3

---

## RULES

- Never use music with recognizable melody — purely textural/ambient
- Never let music compete with voice in any frequency
- Music choice matches the video's emotional arc, not just the topic
