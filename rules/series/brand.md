## LOGO & WATERMARK

- Position: bottom right corner
- Offset: 48px from right edge, 48px from bottom edge
- Size: no larger than 80px height
- Opacity: 60%
- Never obscured by other elements
- Never animated — static for the full video duration
- Color: always the teal accent `#2A7B7B` on light, or cream `#F5F0E8` on dark backgrounds

---

## BRAND MARK RULES

- The brand mark appears in every single video
- It appears from frame 1 to last frame — no fade in, no fade out
- It is never repositioned based on other content
- If an element would overlap it, move the element, not the brand mark

## WHAT BRAND MEANS IN THIS SYSTEM

Brand is not the logo.
Brand is the feeling a viewer has after watching two videos in a row and immediately knowing they're from the same creator.
Every file in /rules/style contributes to brand.
Every file in /rules/series contributes to brand.
Violating any of them is a brand violation, not just a style error.
