# REEL.md — Project Studio 1.0
## Format: Instagram Reel — 9:16

---

## CANVAS SPECS
```
Width:        1080px
Height:       1920px
Frame rate:   30fps
Max duration: 90 seconds
Codec:        h264
```

---

## SAFE ZONES — CRITICAL
Instagram overlays UI elements. Nothing important outside these bounds:
```
Top:    150px clearance — Instagram header, profile info
Bottom: 200px clearance — like/comment/share buttons, caption
Left:   60px margin
Right:  60px margin

Safe content area: 960px × 1570px centered
```

---

## TEXT SIZE HIERARCHY (reel)
```
Title:     72–80px, bold weight
Subtitle:  48–52px, medium weight
Body:      40–44px, regular weight
Label:     32–36px, medium weight
Caption:   28px, regular weight
```
Never go below 28px — unreadable on mobile.

---

## BRAND MARK POSITION
Bottom right: 48px from right, 220px from bottom (above Instagram safe zone)

---

## PACING FOR REEL
- 30 seconds: 3 scenes × 10s
- 60 seconds: 5 scenes × 12s
- 90 seconds: 7 scenes × ~13s

Reels get 3 seconds to hook or they scroll.
First frame must communicate the topic instantly.
No slow intro. No title card with music. Into the content immediately.

Label:     28–32px, medium

## INTRO CARD
- Duration: 3 seconds
- Content: topic title + subtle background animation
- No logo intro — straight to topic title

## OUTRO CARD
- Duration: 5 seconds
- Content: key takeaway line (1 sentence), brand mark
- Fade to dark background `#2C2416` as base

Bottom right: 48px from right edge, 48px from bottom
